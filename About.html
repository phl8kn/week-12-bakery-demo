Our story

Marie and Daniel Okafor opened Corner Bakery in 1998 with one oven and three kinds of bread. Today we bake more than twenty breads and pastries a day, but we still start every sourdough the night before.

How we bake

Our sourdough ferments for 18 hours. We use Ontario flour from a mill near Almonte and bake on stone, which gives the crust its colour and crackle.

Our team

Eight bakers, four counter staff, and one very old sourdough starter named Gerald. Who does what:

  • Marie: bread and the sourdough starter
  • Daniel: pastries and cakes
  • Aisha: the counter and coffee
