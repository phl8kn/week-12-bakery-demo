Fresh bread and pastries on Bank Street

Corner Bakery has baked bread in downtown Ottawa since 1998. Every loaf is shaped by hand and baked in our stone oven before the doors open at 7 a.m.

Learn to bake your own loaf in our evening sourdough class, or browse the full bread and pastry menu.
