Sourdough baking class

Spend an evening in our kitchen and learn to mix, shape and bake a sourdough loaf. Classes run every Thursday from 6 to 9 p.m.

Next class: Thursday, December 3, 2026, 6 to 9 p.m.

What you'll learn

In three hours you will practise:

  • Feeding and keeping a starter
  • Mixing, folding and shaping
  • Baking in a home oven

Price and booking

$85 per person, all ingredients included. Groups are limited to eight. Book through our contact form.
